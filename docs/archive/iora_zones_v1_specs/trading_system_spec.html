01Core Principle — Fractal Push & Pull

Every timeframe's price movement is either pushing toward a target zone or being pulled back by a higher timeframe's gravitational zones. This is fractal — the same mechanic repeats at every parent-child timeframe pair in the cascade.

At any moment, every TF has a direction determined by its parent TF. The parent's zones define the gravitational boundaries — the magnets — that the child TF pushes toward or reverses from. When a parent TF creates a zone, the child TF's job is to push price through its own zones to fulfill (or fail to fulfill) the parent's structural move.

The framework answers three questions mechanically, at every TF, at all times:

The Three Questions

1. Where are we? — Which parent zone are we inside? What role does that zone have (push, continuation, pullback)?

2. Where are we going? — What is the nearest target zone (magnet) that the current push is driving toward?

3. What confirms the move is over? — What structural event (zone break, trendline break, CHoCH cascade) tells us the push is exhausted and the pull begins?

03Zone Creation — Heikin-Ashi Transitions

Zones are the atomic unit of the system. Every structural event — every push, pullback, and reversal — is expressed through zones. A zone is born from a Heikin-Ashi color transition, lives until broken or expired, and its classification tells you where it sits in the structural sequence.

Trigger Events

Zones are created by HA color transitions detected via request.security() on each enabled timeframe.

TransitionZone TypeMeaning
Red → Blue HA close ≥ HA open after red run DEMAND Sellers exhausted, buyers stepping in
Blue → Red HA close < HA open after blue run SUPPLY Buyers exhausted, sellers stepping in

Zone Boundaries (ORIZ Spec)

The zone is not just the transition candle — it spans the entire HA run that preceded it. The run is scanned backwards (up to 50 bars) to find the structural extreme.

Zone TypeTopBottom
SUPPLY OHLC high of the run (structural extreme — highest high in the blue run) HA low of transition candle (order-block edge)
DEMAND HA high of transition candle (order-block edge) OHLC low of the run (structural extreme — lowest low in the red run)

Doji Handling

If the transition candle is a doji (body/range < 5%), the zone boundary uses the current candle's HA extreme instead of the previous candle's. This prevents tiny-body candles from creating unreasonably narrow zones.

Zone Classification (HH / LH / HL / LL)

When a new zone fires, compare it to the previous zone of the same type on the same TF. One rule, every timeframe, every zone:

Supply Zone Classification
New supply top > previous supply top  →  HH  (structural — trend extends)
New supply top ≤ previous supply top  →  LH  (corrective — trend weakening)
Demand Zone Classification
New demand bot < previous demand bot  →  LL  (structural — trend extends)
New demand bot ≥ previous demand bot  →  HL  (corrective — trend weakening)
ClassificationMeaningStructural ImplicationStructure Event
HH New high exceeded previous high Bullish trend continuing If HH zone broken → BOS
LH New high failed to exceed previous Bullish momentum fading If LH zone broken → CHoCH
LL New low exceeded previous low Bearish trend continuing If LL zone broken → BOS
HL New low held above previous Bearish momentum fading If HL zone broken → CHoCH

Zone Lifecycle

Creation

A zone is created when the HA color transition fires and passes validation: ztop > zbot (valid range), zone age within TF-specific max age window, and the zone is pushed to the per-TF zone array.

Active State

While active, the zone box extends to the right. Labeled with TF, type (S/D), and classification (HH/LH/HL/LL).

Break Detection — Body Close Only

Critical Rule — Break = Body Close Only

A zone is broken only by a candle body close through it:

Supply broken: close > zone.top — price body-closed above supply

Demand broken: close < zone.bottom — price body-closed below demand

Wicks through a zone are NOT breaks. A wick into a zone is a liquidity sweep — the zone remains active.

Deletion

A zone is removed when: (1) Broken — body close through it, immediate deletion. (2) Expired — zone age exceeds TF-specific max age. (3) Count overflow — more than 20 supply or 20 demand zones per TF, oldest removed first. Broken zones are deleted immediately. They do not persist as ghost zones.

04Zone Roles — Push, Continuation, Pullback, Reversal

Every zone, in addition to its type (supply/demand) and classification (HH/LH/HL/LL), has a role that describes its function within the parent-child cascade. The role determines what the zone means for directional bias and target identification.

Zone roles are not intrinsic properties — they are relational. A zone's role depends on where it sits relative to its parent TF's structure. The same zone can look different depending on which parent-child relationship you're examining.

Push Zone PUSH

Definition — Push Zone

The singular child zone, created inside the parent zone that produced the parent's last HH/LL. It is the zone that initiated the structural move at the parent level — the deepest origin point of the push.

There is exactly one push zone per parent structural move.

Identification Rule

GIVEN:  Parent TF created zone Z (which made the parent's last HH or LL)
THEN:   The child zone created INSIDE zone Z that initiated the push
        to break through and create the parent's new HH/LL
IS:     The push zone for this parent-child pair
Example — GBPUSD MN → W

Monthly demand zone created. Inside this MN demand, a Weekly demand zone is created — this Weekly demand is the PUSH zone. It drives price up through Weekly supply zones to eventually make a new W HH above the previous MN LH. The MN demand zone is the parent; the W demand inside it is the push zone.

Push Zone as Magnet

The push zone is a gravitational magnet. When the child TF pulls back counter to the parent's direction, the push zone is where price is being pulled toward. The pullback resolves either by reaching the push zone or by confirming a new push zone before reaching it.

Push Zone Disqualification

Rule — Wick ≠ Push

If the child TF's wick reaches the prior push zone boundary but does not body-close through it, the move does NOT qualify as creating a new push zone. Only a body-close break of the boundary extends the push.

Continuation Zone CONTINUATION

Definition — Continuation Zone

Additional child zones created after the push zone, in the same direction, extending the HH higher or LL lower. They ride the push but did not initiate it.

There can be multiple continuation zones per push.

Identification Rule

GIVEN:  Push zone P exists for this parent-child pair
AND:    A new child zone fires in the SAME direction as P
AND:    This zone is created ABOVE the push zone (for bullish) 
        or BELOW the push zone (for bearish)
AND:    It extends the structural sequence (new HH or new LL)
THEN:   This zone is a CONTINUATION zone
Example — GBPUSD W → D

Weekly push demand established. Daily creates a new demand zone above the MN demand top and above the W push demand — this is the first CONTINUATION zone. As price continues up, additional Daily demand zones that make higher lows are further continuation zones, stacking the structure upward toward the eventual W HH.

Pullback Zone PULLBACK

Definition — Pullback Zone

Child zones that push counter to the parent's direction, moving price back toward the parent's push zone. The parent's push zone is the magnet pulling the LTFs back into it.

Pullback zones remain within the existing parent structure — they do not break the parent's LH/HL structural level.

Identification Rule

GIVEN:  Parent is bullish (last parent zone = HH or HL demand, pushing up)
AND:    Child creates SUPPLY zones (counter to parent direction)
AND:    These supply zones push price DOWN toward the parent's push zone
AND:    No break of the parent's structural HL level
THEN:   These child supply zones are PULLBACK zones

GIVEN:  Parent is bearish (last parent zone = LL or LH supply, pushing down)
AND:    Child creates DEMAND zones (counter to parent direction)  
AND:    These demand zones push price UP toward the parent's push zone
AND:    No break of the parent's structural LH level
THEN:   These child demand zones are PULLBACK zones
Example — GBPUSD W → D (bearish pullback)

Weekly made its HH, creating a W supply zone at the top. Now price turns down. Daily creates LH supply zones pushing price lower — these Daily LH S zones are PULLBACK zones. They are pulling price back into the Weekly push demand zone. Each Daily LH supply is a "sell here" magnet — bearish bias at the Daily level until the Weekly pullback resolves.

Pullback Resolution

A pullback resolves in one of two ways:

A
Price reaches the parent push zone. Pullback complete. Parent direction resumes from the push zone. The child zone that forms here becomes the new push zone if a new parent HH/LL is made.
B
The last child pullback zone is breached (body close through). This confirms a new push zone in the parent's direction — the pullback has been rejected and the parent push is extending again without reaching the original push zone.

Reversal Zone REVERSAL

Definition — Reversal Zone

When a pullback goes deep enough that the grandchild breaks the parent's structural zone (the parent's LH or HL), the pullback becomes a reversal. The child zone that drove the break of the continuation zone is the reversal zone.

A reversal zone marks the start of a new parent leg — the HL or LH that will push in the opposite direction.

Identification Rule

GIVEN:  Child is pulling back (counter to parent direction)
AND:    Grandchild zones driving the pullback break through 
        the parent's LH supply zone (bearish) or HL demand zone (bullish)
THEN:   The pullback has become a REVERSAL
AND:    The child zone that drove the structural break = REVERSAL zone
AND:    Price reverses from the reversal zone
AND:    A new parent leg (LH or HL) begins
Key Distinction — Pullback vs Reversal

Pullback: child moves counter to parent direction but stays within parent structure. No parent LH/HL broken.

Reversal: grandchild breaks the parent's structural zone (LH/HL). The pullback has gone too deep — parent structure has shifted.

The transition point: grandchild breaks parent structure = pullback becomes reversal.

Zone Role Summary

RoleDirection vs ParentCountFunction
PUSH Same Exactly 1 Initiates parent's structural move. Magnet for pullbacks.
CONTINUATION Same 0 or more Extends the push. Stacks structure higher/lower.
PULLBACK Counter 0 or more Retraces toward push zone. "Sell/buy here" magnets at child level.
REVERSAL Counter 0 or 1 Breaks parent structure. Starts new parent leg (LH/HL).

05Directional Bias — Top-Down Read

At any moment, the system reads direction top-down. Each parent-child pair is evaluated independently using the same logic. The cascade creates a composite bias from MN down to execution TF.

The Mechanical Read

1
MN: What was the last MN zone created? Supply → macro bearish. Demand → macro bullish. This sets the ultimate gravitational frame.
2
W: Where is the last W zone relative to MN? If W supply is inside MN supply push zone → bearish, ride down. If W demand is inside MN demand push zone → bullish, ride up. If W demand sits below tested MN supply after MN demand was created → expect bullish reversal, push up to break MN LH.
3
D: Where is the last D zone relative to W? Same logic. If D supply inside W supply (at W HH) → bearish at Daily level. Daily LH S zones = pullback zones pulling price into W push demand. Every D supply = "sell here" magnet until W structure shifts.
4
H4: Where is the last H4 zone relative to D? Same logic. H4 zones inside D LH supply = the push zones driving the D pullback lower. H4 continuation zones track the push progress.
Example — GBPUSD Current Read (as shown in charts)

MN: Last MN zone = demand. Macro bullish.

W: W push demand inside MN demand → pushed up to make W HH above MN LH. W supply created at top. W continuation demand formed above push zone. Now in W pullback (price pulling back toward W push demand).

D: Inside W supply at top, Daily created LH supply zones pushing down. These are PULLBACK zones at the W level. Daily bias = bearish (sell at every D LH S) until W pullback resolves.

H4: H4 zones inside the D LH S drive the push lower. H4 supply zones = push zones for the D pullback leg. When H4 pushes up from D trendline but fails to break the last H4 bullish continuation → confirms continued push down into W push zone.

06Trendline Confirmation Layer

Trendlines provide an early warning system for push exhaustion and pullback resolution. They are drawn mechanically from zone events, not subjectively.

Trendline Construction

Rule — Trendline Anchor Points

A trendline is drawn from the push zone through the continuation zones at the same TF.

Bullish trendline: Connects the push zone's demand bottom to the continuation zones' demand bottoms (rising support).

Bearish trendline: Connects the push zone's supply top to the continuation zones' supply tops (falling resistance).

Trendline Break Signals

EventMeaning
Grandchild pushes from TF trendline into last child continuation zone → fails to break it Confirms parent direction continues. Push resumes toward target.
Grandchild breaks last child continuation zone above/below trendline Early warning: pullback may be exhausting. Watch for reversal confirmation.
Child trendline from push zone is body-close broken Push structure has broken. Pullback or reversal in progress at this TF level.
Example — H4 and Daily Trendlines (from charts)

H4 pushes up from the Daily trendline, created from the D continuation zone. H4 reaches the last H4 bullish continuation zone but fails to break it. This confirms the continued push down — into the Weekly push zone. The Daily bearish trendline (from D LH S push zone through D LH S continuation zones) remains intact.

07Pullback-to-Reversal Transition

The transition from pullback to reversal is the most critical structural event in the system. It tells you when a counter-move is no longer "coming back" — it has broken structure and started a new leg.

Two-Layer Confirmation

The end of a pullback is not confirmed at the pullback TF itself. It requires confirmation from one level deeper (grandchild), then validation at the child level:

1
Grandchild CHoCH. The grandchild TF creates a LH (if parent was bullish) or HL (if parent was bearish) — signaling that the child's counter-push is exhausting.
2
Child pullback zone broken. A child pullback zone (the LH S or HL D) gets body-close broken — confirming the pullback is over. If this break also breaks the parent's structural level (parent LH/HL), the pullback has become a reversal.
3
New push zone confirmed. The demand/supply zone that price reversed from becomes the new push zone. A new parent leg begins.
The Transition Test

Still a pullback if: child moves counter to parent but does NOT break the parent's LH/HL. Grandchild structure still aligned with child's counter-direction.

Becomes a reversal when: grandchild breaks the parent's structural zone (LH or HL). At this point, the child zone that drove the break = reversal zone. A new parent leg (HL/LH) begins from the reversal zone.

Post-Reversal Cycle

After a reversal is confirmed:

1
The reversal zone becomes the origin of a new parent leg (HL or LH).
2
A new parent zone is created from this leg — this new zone now becomes the magnet for the child TFs.
3
The child TFs are pulled through their own push zones toward this new parent zone.
4
The child TFs create counter-moves (pullbacks) that feed back up into the parent structure.
5
The cycle repeats. Up and down the timeframes, endlessly.
