# Empire Naming Exploration
# Navneutforsking for Imperiet

**Purpose / Formaal:** Explore creative name alternatives for the holding structure and its subsidiaries that reflect the founder's core philosophy — continuous improvement, breath and body, mindfulness, natural energy, working in the shadows to build a better world, and empowering every person to become their best self.

**Inspirations / Inspirasjoner:** Kaizen (continuous improvement), Joe Dispenza (rewiring the mind), David Eagleman (neuroplasticity, hidden brain), Jim Rohn (personal development, discipline), breath work, mind-body training, natural energy, the universe, shadow work, relentless persistence.

---

## Naming Themes

### Theme 1: ARIS (Current — Greek: Best/Excellence)
Strong, established, directly communicates the philosophy. Already in use.

**Pros:** Recognized globally. Embodies the iterative, never-stop-improving ethos.
**Cons:** Common in business. Doesn't capture the deeper spiritual/shadow/energy dimensions.

---

### Theme 2: MERIDIAN (Energy Lines / Peak)
From both anatomy (energy meridians in the body) and geography (the highest point the sun reaches). Bridges body, energy, and peak performance.

```
MERIDIAN HOLDINGS AS (Morselskap)
  |
  +-- MERIDIAN CAPITAL AS             (Algoritmisk Trading)
  +-- MERIDIAN ASSET MANAGEMENT AS    (Hedgefond / Fondsforvaltning)
  +-- MERIDIAN VENTURES AS            (Aksje- & Ventureportefolje)
  +-- MERIDIAN EIENDOM AS             (Eiendom)
  +-- MERIDIAN BYGG AS                (Bygg & Utvikling)
  +-- MERIDIAN TECH AS                (Teknologi & Infrastruktur)
  +-- MERIDIAN ADVISORY AS            (Raadgivning)
  +-- MERIDIAN FOUNDATION             (Stiftelse)
```

**Why it works:** Energy lines in the body (breath, mindfulness). Peak/zenith (becoming your best self). Universal — works in English, Norwegian, and internationally. Professional yet carries hidden depth for those who know.

## Hybrid Approach: Mix Names Across Entities

You don't have to use the same root for every entity. The holding company is the umbrella — subsidiaries can have distinct identities.

```
MERIDIAN HOLDINGS AS (or PNEUMA GROUP AS)
  |
  +-- ARIS CAPITAL AS               (Keep the trading arm as Aris — it's the engine)
  +-- MERIDIAN ASSET MANAGEMENT AS    (Professional, institutional-facing)
  +-- AETHER VENTURES AS              (Venture/PE — cosmic bets on the future)
  +-- STILLE EIENDOM AS               (Quiet real estate empire)
  +-- STILLE BYGG AS                  (Silent builder)
  +-- PRAXIS TECH AS                  (Technology through disciplined action)
  +-- SENTIENT ADVISORY AS            (Awareness-driven consulting)
  +-- INDRE KRAFT STIFTELSE           (Inner strength foundation — pure Norwegian)
```

## Founder's Decision Framework

**If you want maximum "shadow worker" energy:**
UMBRA or STILLE — the empire nobody sees until it's everywhere.

**If you want the breath/energy/body connection:**
PNEUMA or MERIDIAN — the life force that powers everything.

**If you want cosmic/universal scope:**
AETHER — the invisible fabric connecting all your ventures.

**If you want pure Norwegian identity:**
STILLE or INDRE KRAFT — deeply personal, culturally rooted.

**If you want institutional credibility from day one:**
MERIDIAN or SENTIENT — professional, modern, scalable.

**If you want to keep the Aris engine but elevate the umbrella:**
Use MERIDIAN/PNEUMA/AETHER as the holding name, keep ARIS CAPITAL for the trading arm.

## Next Steps

3. **Check domain availability** (.no, .com, .io)
4. **Trademark search** via Patentstyret (Norwegian IP office)
