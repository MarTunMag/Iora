## Phase A — Going Real (Now)

You need exactly two things: an accountant and possibly an initial tax conversation.

### 1. Accountant (Regnskapsforer)

**Call first:**

| Priority | Firm | Why | Contact |
|----------|------|-----|---------|
| 1 | **Leinonen Norway** | Oslo-based, understands USD/NOK workflows and international brokers. Ideal first accountant for a trader with foreign broker | leinonen.eu/nor |
| 2 | **ybiN AS** | Deep cross-border expertise. Good if you plan non-Norwegian entities later | ybin.no |
| 3 | **Norus Regnskapsbyrå** | Simple, full-service for small companies. Good if you just need basic AS bookkeeping | norus-regnskap.no |

**What to ask:**
- "I do algorithmic forex/CFD trading via an international broker (ICMarkets). I need help with annual accounts and tax filing."
- "I generate trade reports in JSON/CSV format with NOK-converted P&L. Can you work with structured data files?"
- "At what capital level would you recommend forming an AS?"

**Expected cost:** NOK 10,000-30,000/year for basic AS accounting.

### 2. Tax Advisor (Skatteradgiver) — Initial Consultation

**Call first:**

| Priority | Firm | Why | Contact |
|----------|------|-----|---------|
| 1 | **Accace Norway** | Cost-effective compliance-focused tax support. Good for Phase A-B | accace.com |
| 2 | **CMS Kluge** | Top-tier but more expensive. Better for Phase B when you need holding structure design | cms.law/nor |

**What to ask:**
- "I'm a Norwegian resident doing automated forex/CFD trading. Is my activity classified as kapitalinntekt or naeringsinntekt?"
- "When does it make sense to form a Holding AS + Trading AS structure?"
- "What do I need to report to Skatteetaten for this tax year?"

**Expected cost:** NOK 3,000-10,000 for an initial 1-2 hour consultation.

## Phase B — AS Formation (NOK 500K-5M)

### 3. Business Lawyer (Forretningsadvokat)

**Call first:**

| Priority | Firm | Why | Contact |
|----------|------|-----|---------|
| 1 | **CMS Kluge** | Best balance of quality and practicality. Combined corporate + tax expertise. One-stop shop | cms.law/nor |
| 2 | **SANDS** | 220+ professionals, national coverage. Good for straightforward AS setup and shareholder agreements | sands.no |

**What to ask:**
- "I need to set up a Holding AS that owns 100% of a Trading AS. Can you handle entity formation, vedtekter, and shareholder agreements?"
- "What management fee agreement should I have between Holding and Trading AS?"
- "What are the Bronnoysund filing requirements?"

**Expected cost:** NOK 15,000-40,000 for entity setup + agreements.

### 4. Insurance Broker (Forsikringsmegler)

**Call first:**

| Priority | Firm | Why | Contact |
|----------|------|-----|---------|
| 1 | **Soderberg & Partners** | Nordic's largest independent broker. Corporate + personal packages | soderbergpartners.no |

**What to ask:**
- "I'm forming a small trading AS. I need D&O insurance and general business liability. What's the minimum package?"
- "I also run critical IT infrastructure for trading. Do you offer cyber insurance?"

**Expected cost for quotes:** Free. Insurance premiums: NOK 10,000-30,000/year initially.
