# Rule 10 — The D-Level Cycle (5 Phases)

> **The complete post-exhaustion sequence from terminal bottom to next D LL.**
> Every tradeable moment maps to one of these phases.

---

## The Five Phases

### Phase A — H4 Demand Confirmation

```
What happens:
  H1 demand created at bottom (inside H4 demand)
  → Push up → H1 supply #8 created
  → Push back down → breaks that H1 demand
  → Pushdown reaches M15/M5 demand at lowest H1 demand bottom
  → H4 demand confirmed as LIVE

What to look for:
  H4 panel: H4 demand zone present below broken D LL
  H1 panel: Break-retest cycle completing inside H4 demand
  M15 panel: M15 demand at the very bottom = entry zone
```

### Phase B — Reversal Into Boundary

```
What happens:
  Price pushes up from confirmed H4 demand
  → Into the reversal zone (H1 boundary zone from Phase A)
  → This is the bullish reversal leg

What to look for:
  H1 panel: New H1 demand zones forming (HL sequence = bullish)
  Boundary zone: Price approaching / entering it
  M15 panel: M15 demand zones inside H1 demand = continuation entries
```

### Phase C — H4 Supply at Reversal Zone

```
What happens:
  At the boundary zone, price reverses
  → Creates new H4 supply zone inside/near boundary
  → This confirms D LH (H4 HH capped below D supply)

What to look for:
  H4 panel: New H4 supply zone appearing
  D panel: New D LH confirmed (H4 supply top < prev D supply top)
  H1 panel: H1 LH sequence starting (bearish structure)
```

### Phase D — Aggressive D LL Push

```
What happens:
  D LH confirmed → aggressive push down begins
  → H1 supply zones broken aggressively WITHOUT retests
  → Entries shift to M5/M15 supply zones directly
  → This is Mode B continuation trading

What to look for:
  H1 panel: H1 supply zones breaking fast (no stair-step retest)
  M15 panel: M15 supply zones = aggressive entry points
  M5 panel: M5 supply zones = precision execution
```

### Phase E — New D LL

```
What happens:
  M5/M15 → H1 supply chain pushes price to new D LL
  → Cycle restarts at Phase A

What to look for:
  D panel: New D LL printing
  H4 panel: Will a new H4 demand form below it? → next terminal
```

---

## Which Phase Am I In?

```
Terminal exhaustion active + H4 demand confirming    → Phase A
Price pushing up from H4 demand toward boundary      → Phase B
H4 supply forming at boundary / D LH confirming      → Phase C
D LH confirmed + aggressive push down                → Phase D
New D LL printing                                     → Phase E → back to A
```

---

## Trading Mode Per Phase

| Phase | Entry Mode | What to Trade |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| A | Reversal (Path A) | Long from M15 demand @ terminal H4 demand |
| B | Continuation longs | Long from H1 demand retests (HL sequence) |
| C | Bearish trigger (Path D) | Short from M15 supply @ H1 unbroken supply |
| D | Aggressive continuation (Mode B) | Short from M5/M15 supply zones |
| E | Terminal detection | Watch for next H4 counter-zone |

---

## The Mirror

Everything above is the bearish-to-bullish-to-bearish cycle. The bullish-to-bearish-to-bullish cycle is the exact mirror — swap supply/demand, HH/LL, long/short.
