Quick reference checklist

  1. MN/W strategic: Where are MN and W supply/demand boundaries? Is price approaching a strategic extreme? These are the absolute ceiling/floor.
  2. D macro: Latest D supply = HH or LH? Latest D demand = LL or HL? Which macro direction? Are W boundaries framing the D range?
  3. H4 context: Inside D supply or demand zone? H4 zone classification? H4 CHoCH sourcing from D zone? What context_mode?
  4. H1 zone count: 1–5 = enter. 6 = last entry. 7–8 = hold only. Terminal? H4 counter-zone below broken D LL?
  5. M15/M5 signal: M15 TL break? M15 CHoCH inside relevant H1 zone? Direction? M5 zone = Mode B entry?
  6. Entry trigger: M1 CHoCH inside M5 zone, right direction, body close, external swing.
  7. Validity: CHoCH sourcing from HTF zone? Wick-only = skip. Internal swing = skip.
  8. Target stack: Next unbroken OB on same/higher TF = immediate target. Breaker/mitigation zones between entry and target = pullback management levels. Child TF CHoCH at target = "is it done?"

Macro bias confirmation

The macro bias is the confirmed directional filter. It is triggered by an M5 trendline break while price sits inside an H4 or D supply/demand zone. Once set, it persists until the opposite confirmation fires.

Bearish confirmation (flip to SHORT)
  • ☐ M5 bullish TL broken (ascending support failed)
  • ☐ (Optional: M1 bullish TL also broken)
  • ☐ Price inside H4 supply OR D supply zone
  • ☐ terminal_exhaustion_bull is NOT active
  • → macro_bias = BEARISH (−1)
Bullish confirmation (flip to LONG)
  • ☐ M5 bearish TL broken (descending resistance failed)
  • ☐ (Optional: M1 bearish TL also broken)
  • ☐ Price inside H4 demand OR D demand zone
  • ☐ terminal_exhaustion_bear is NOT active
  • → macro_bias = BULLISH (+1)
Terminal gate: Don't flip BULLISH when terminal_exhaustion_bear is active (H4 demand below D LL = bounces are temporary). Don't flip BEARISH when terminal_exhaustion_bull is active. This prevents false reversals at terminal zones.

D-Level cycle — 5 phases

The complete post-exhaustion sequence from terminal bottom to next D LL. Every tradeable moment maps to one of these phases.

A
H4 Demand Confirmation

H1 demand created at bottom (inside H4 demand) → push up → H1 supply #8 → push back down → breaks H1 demand → pushdown reaches M15/M5 demand → H4 demand confirmed LIVE. Entry: M15 demand @ H4 demand.

B
Reversal Into Boundary

Price pushes up from confirmed H4 demand into the reversal zone (H1 boundary zone). This is the bullish reversal leg. Entry: H1 demand retests (HL sequence).

C
H4 Supply at Boundary — D LH Confirms

At the boundary zone, price reverses → new H4 supply forms → D LH confirmed (H4 HH capped below D supply). Entry: Short from M15 supply @ H1 supply.

D
Aggressive D LL Push (Mode B)

D LH confirmed → aggressive push down. H1 supply zones broken without retests. Entry: M5/M15 supply zones directly.

E
New D LL — Cycle Restarts

Push reaches new D LL → cycle restarts at Phase A. Check: will a new H4 demand form below?

"Which phase am I in?" decision tree

Terminal exhaustion active + H4 demand confirming    → Phase A
Price pushing up from H4 demand toward boundary      → Phase B
H4 supply forming at boundary / D LH confirming      → Phase C
D LH confirmed + aggressive push down                → Phase D
New D LL printing                                     → Phase E → back to A

Zone Tracking & Reversal Targets

Core principle: As price pushes in one direction, always track the last created zone at each level (D/H4/H1/M15). The zone that caused the CHoCH at each level becomes the reversal target for the level above. This is how you know WHERE to exit and WHERE to enter the next trade.

The "last created zone" principle

As price pushes in one direction, each timeframe creates zones along the way. The freshest zone is the one that matters — always update to the newest.

As price pushes DOWN through…Track…Why it matters
D zonesLast created D supplyThis is the reversal target when D turns
H4 zonesLast created H4 supplyThis is the D LH zone when H4 reverses up
H1 zonesLast created H1 supplyThis is the H4 reversal target
M15 zonesLast created M15 supplyEntry/exit precision inside H1

CHoCH zone = reversal target for parent TF

The H1 zone that caused the H4 CHoCH = your reversal target. When the H4 makes its HL, mark the H1 supply zone that was active when that happened. That's where you exit longs and look for shorts on the way back up.

H4 is pushing down (making H4 LLs) → H1 supply zones are being created as price descends → At some point, H1 makes an HL instead of LL → This H1 HL = H4 HL (CHoCH at H4 level) → The H1 SUPPLY zone that was last created before this HL = YOUR REVERSAL TARGET

The "don't get tricked by H4 HL" warning

Critical: After a D LL break, the FIRST H4 zone on the opposite side is NOT your reversal target yet. The H4 HL looks like the market is reversing, but the D LL just broke — the H4 HL is just a correction in the push, not a reversal. You need the H4 HL to break first to CONFIRM the D LL. Only after that break is complete do you have a clean reversal target. 
After D LL break:
  → Track the HIGHEST H4 supply created during the push
  → The H4 HL must break first (pushing further down)
  → The H4 supply that caused the H4 HL break
    = the zone that pushed down to break D LL
    = YOUR D LH reversal target when price pushes back up

D LH reversal zone identification

The D LH reversal zone is specifically: the H1 supply zone that was created as the H4 pushed to break the H4 HL, which then pushed to break the D LL, confirming the new D LL + W LL. When price pushes back up to this zone → H4 HH forms (but below last H4 supply = D LH, not D HH) → confirm reversal INSIDE this zone → short entries from here.

Weekly context check at D LL

After riding to a new D LL, check: Is this D LL inside the W demand from the last W push up?

YES → W HL (weekly correction)

The D pullback reverses → continues to make new W HH → then W HL → new Monthly HH or LH. Buy the D LL for W continuation.

NO → W impulse continues

New W LL extends → look for next D demand below as target. Sell the D HL for W continuation down.

Full 8-step mechanical sequence

1
Identify Terminal

D LL broken, H4 demand below, H1 count 5+3 → Terminal confirmed.

2
Enter Long

M15 demand @ H4 demand (M15@H4 nesting) → M1 CHoCH (HL) inside M5 @ this M15 demand → LONG ENTRY.

3
Track Reversal Target

Mark: H1 supply that created H4 HL break (= H4 impulse D break zone). This is where you will EXIT LONGS.

4
Ride to Target

Price pushes up through H1 zones → H4 HL → H4 HH (but below last H4 supply = D LH) → approaching the marked H1 supply.

5
Exit Longs at D LH Zone

Price enters the H1 supply marked in Step 3 → EXIT LONGS (TP).

6
Enter Short

Same zone: H1 supply = D LH reversal zone → sell limit (Path D) + M1 CHoCH add-on shorts (Path E).

7
Ride D Push Down

D LH confirmed → aggressive push to new D LL + W LL → Mode B continuation shorts from M5/M15 supply.

8
Check Weekly Context

At new D LL: Is this inside W demand from last W push? YES = W HL, prepare for reversal. NO = W continuation, ride further.
