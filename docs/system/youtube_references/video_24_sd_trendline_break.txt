TRENDLINE DRAWING RULES:
- Connect swing points along the price action (at least 2-3 connections)
- Author prefers 3 connections for validity, but sometimes uses 2
- Draw trendline on the pullback/retracement within the demand/supply zone

TRENDLINE BREAK RULES:
- 4 ways to enter a trendline break (from aggressive to conservative):
  1. ANTICIPATE the breakout: enter at double bottom/rejection before break (most aggressive)
  2. AS SOON AS price breaks the trend line, enter immediately
  3. Wait for CANDLESTICK CLOSE above/below the trend line, then enter
  4. Wait for SWING HIGH/LOW BREAK after the trendline break (most conservative)
- First 2 methods are aggressive; last 2 are better for beginners (more confirmation)
- In downtrend: wait for price to close below trend line
- In uptrend: wait for price to close above trend line

ZONE-TO-TRENDLINE INTERACTION:
- Smart money creates supply/demand zones: identified by 3-4+ consecutive same-direction candles of decent size
- Demand zone: area where smart money entered with large buys (3+ consecutive green candles)
- Supply zone: area where smart money entered with large sells (3+ consecutive red candles)
- Price returns to zone = strong possibility smart money pushes price again
- Trendline drawn WITHIN the zone's pullback/retest area
- Zone provides the context (where), trendline break provides the trigger (when)
- Flow: identify zone -> wait for price to return -> draw trendline on pullback -> enter on break

PIVOT IDENTIFICATION RULES:
- Swing highs and swing lows used as reference points
- Break of swing high after trendline break = strongest confirmation (method 4)
- Double bottom/top patterns within zones signal potential breakout

ENTRY/EXIT RULES AROUND TRENDLINE BREAKS:
- Entry: one of 4 methods above depending on risk tolerance
- Stop loss options:
  - Tight: below the breakout candle
  - Wider: below the demand zone / above the supply zone
- Target: ride to the recent high/low; aim for at least 2:1 R:R
- Beginners should use confirmation methods (candle close or swing break)
- More experienced traders can anticipate or enter on immediate break

MULTI-TF RULES:
- Live example shown on 5-minute chart (Nasdaq 100)
- Concepts apply across timeframes
- Channel/triangle breakouts combined with S/D zones on intraday charts
- No explicit HTF/LTF cascade described, but S/D zones are identified on trading TF
