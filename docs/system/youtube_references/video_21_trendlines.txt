TRENDLINE DRAWING RULES:
- A trend line is a diagonal line connecting two or more swing highs or swing lows
- Acts as support during an uptrend (buyers protect it), resistance during downtrend (sellers defend it)
- Must connect at least 2 points, preferably 3 or more
- More touches = stronger trend line
- Do not force the line - follow natural swing highs and lows
- In an uptrend, connect swing lows (lowest points where price temporarily reverses upward)

TRENDLINE BREAK RULES:
- When price breaks a trend line, it signals a potential trend reversal
- Do NOT jump in immediately on the break - wait for retest
- Break of trend line means buyers losing control, sellers taking over (in uptrend break)
- The break is the first clue of reversal, but requires confirmation

ZONE-TO-TRENDLINE INTERACTION:
- Supply zone: area where sellers aggressively entered, pushing price down (near market high/resistance)
- Demand zone: area where buyers take control, pushing price higher (like a floor)
- Supply/demand zones form where price consolidates briefly before a big movement (institutional orders)
- Fresh zones (not retested multiple times) hold more power
- KEY CONCEPT: The intersection of broken trendline retest + supply/demand zone = high probability entry
- Broken trend line acts as resistance; supply zone reinforces selling pressure at the same level
- This intersection creates a "sniper entry" with precision and minimal risk

PIVOT IDENTIFICATION RULES:
- Look for swing highs and swing lows
- Identify areas where price makes a strong move away
- Pay attention to consolidation before big movements

ENTRY/EXIT RULES AROUND TRENDLINE BREAKS:
- Step 1: Identify the trend line (connect swing lows in uptrend)
- Step 2: Spot the supply/demand zone (significant strong move, area where price previously stalled and reversed)
- Step 3: Wait for break of the trend line (shift in momentum)
- Step 4: Wait for retest at the intersection of broken trendline + supply/demand zone
- Confirmation: Look for bearish signals - rejection candle, bearish engulfing, or strong downward momentum
- Stop loss: Just beyond the supply/demand zone
- Target: Favorable risk-to-reward ratio in trend direction
- Confirm entries with pin bars, engulfing candles, or rejection wicks
