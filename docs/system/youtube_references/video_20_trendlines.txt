TRENDLINE DRAWING RULES:
- Ascending trend line: connect lows in an uptrend (market going up)
- Descending trend line: connect highs in a downtrend (drawn above price)
- A trend line needs at least 2 taps; any tap after the 2nd is a trading opportunity
- Trend lines do NOT determine the real trend direction - they are just lines on a chart
- Real trend is determined by the high-low structure of actual price

TRENDLINE BREAK RULES:
- Traditional: break of trend line followed by retest = entry (but author disagrees)
- Author's view: trend line breaks do NOT confirm trend change
- A trend line can break while the market remains in the same trend (based on HL structure)
- The real structural high/low must break to confirm trend change
- Trend line breaks often create "inducement" - trapping both sellers and breakout buyers
- False breaks are common: price breaks trendline, induces buyers, then reverses back to trend

ZONE-TO-TRENDLINE INTERACTION:
- Supply/demand zones near trendlines are the real entry areas (not the trendline itself)
- Supply zone: last consolidation candle before a large impulsive move down
- Demand zone: last consolidation candle before a large impulsive move up
- Prefer zones that created a break in structure (broke past previous low/high) - these are stronger
- Zone above trendline = inducement area where orders accumulate (stop losses + breakout entries)
- The trendline acts as a magnet drawing price to the zone (liquidity inducement)
- Use trendlines to IDENTIFY where supply/demand zones will form, not to trade from directly

PIVOT IDENTIFICATION RULES:
- Track actual price highs and lows (not trendline touches)
- Downtrend: lower highs and lower lows sequence
- Uptrend: higher highs and higher lows sequence
- The significant structural high/low is the key level - trend only changes if this breaks
- As long as price stays under the last lower high = bearish; above last higher low = bullish

ENTRY/EXIT RULES AROUND TRENDLINE BREAKS:
- Do NOT sell the trendline retest directly (can easily get stopped out)
- Do NOT buy the trendline break-retest (market may still be bearish structurally)
- Instead: identify supply/demand zones near the trendline inducement area
- Preferred entry: sell at supply zone above broken trendline (where buyers got trapped)
- Confirmation entry: drop to lower TF (e.g., 30m), wait for break of structure, find supply zone within that move
- Stop loss: above the high (safe) or inside the range if efficient (no imbalances)
- Target: next structural low/high in the trend direction
- Supply zone selection: prefer zone that led to a break of structure (more aggressive selling)

MULTI-TF RULES:
- Works on all timeframes: 1m, 10m, 15m, 30m, 1H, 4H, Daily
- Lower TF (e.g., 30m) used for confirmation entries within HTF supply/demand zones
- Lower TF shows breaks of structure more clearly for sniper entries
- Bullish version is exact mirror of bearish (demand zones instead of supply zones)
