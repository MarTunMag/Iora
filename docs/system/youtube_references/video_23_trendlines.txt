ZONE-TO-TRENDLINE INTERACTION:
- Supply/demand zones are the primary framework (not trendlines)
- Zone refinement: drop to lower TF to find smaller zones inside HTF zones for better R:R
- Demand zone: last candle before impulse up (sideways candle with wicks, little movement before big push)
- Supply zone: last candle before impulse down (consolidation before large bearish move)
- Demand = institutional buying at discount price; Supply = institutional selling at premium price
- Zone drawn as a box around the last candle before the impulse

PIVOT IDENTIFICATION RULES:
- Structural shift: break of structure from LH/LL to HH/HL (or vice versa) signals trend change
- High-low structure tracked on LTF inside zones for confirmation entries
- Standard confirmation: LTF structure changes in agreement with HTF trend direction
  - e.g., in a buy setup: LTF creates new high above previous high = zone has buying strength

ENTRY/EXIT RULES AROUND TRENDLINE BREAKS:
- Zone Refinement Entry (core technique):
  - Identify HTF zone (e.g., 4H demand zone)
  - Drop to LTF (e.g., 1H) to find refined zone inside the HTF zone
  - Place order at the "extreme zone" = furthest zone from price in the leg
  - Extreme zone is safest + best R:R (less likely to get stopped out before reaching it)
  - Stop loss: below the low of the zone (for buys) or above high (for sells)
  - Target: recent swing high/low
  - Example: 4H zone = 3R trade; refined to 1H zone = 5.58R trade (same target)
- Multiple Zones (when LTF shows 2+ zones inside HTF zone):
  - Default: place limit order at extreme zone (furthest from price, best R:R, safest)
  - For closer zone: use "standard confirmation" - wait for LTF structure to shift in trade direction
  - Standard confirmation = LTF creates new high above previous high (for buy) at the zone
  - Then identify new demand inside that confirmation move and place buy limit
- Over-refinement Warning:
  - Do NOT refine too far (e.g., 4H -> 5m) - tiny zones rarely get hit
  - "Two steps down" rule: 4H->1H->30m, Daily->4H->1H, 30m->15m->5m
  - One step down is usually enough to dramatically improve R:R

MULTI-TF RULES:
- Zone refinement = core multi-TF technique
- Step down rule: go 1-2 TFs lower max for zone refinement
  - 4H -> 1H -> 30m (max)
  - Daily -> 4H -> 1H (max)
  - 30m -> 15m -> 5m (max)
- HTF provides the trade premise (zone + direction), LTF provides the refined entry
- Don't go more than 2 steps down - over-refinement leads to missed trades
- Works across all assets (forex, commodities, etc.)
