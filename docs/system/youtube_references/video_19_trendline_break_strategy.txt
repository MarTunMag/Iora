TRENDLINE DRAWING RULES:
- In an uptrend, connect all the higher lows with your trend line
- In a downtrend, connect all the lower highs with your trend line
- Try to get as many touches as possible
- A trend line must have at least 3 touches to be valid (2 is not enough)
- The more times price touches the trend line, the stronger it is (like support/resistance)

TRENDLINE BREAK RULES:
- Wait for price to break through the trend line (do not enter immediately on break)
- Mark the breakout candle with a rectangle/box tool and drag it to the right
- The breakout candle zone becomes the retest zone
- Do NOT enter on the break itself - that leads to fakeouts and losses

ZONE-TO-TRENDLINE INTERACTION:
- The breakout candle creates a zone (rectangle area) for retest entry
- Previous trendline touch points become take-profit levels (Point A, B, C)
- When price breaks through one touch-point level, it targets the next one
- Trendline touch points act as support/resistance levels after the break
- Broken support at touch points flips to resistance (and vice versa) for re-entry

PIVOT IDENTIFICATION RULES:
- Mark every point where price touches and bounces off the trendline
- Label them A (oldest/furthest), B (middle), C (most recent)
- These become the take-profit targets after trendline break

ENTRY/EXIT RULES AROUND TRENDLINE BREAKS:
- Entry: Wait for price to retrace back to the breakout candle zone (rectangle area)
- Confirmation: Look for rejection candles (long wicks, doji) at the rectangle area
- Stop loss: Place above/below the breakout candle rectangle
- TP1 (Point C): Most recent trendline touch point - close 50% here
- TP2 (Point B): Middle trendline touch point - close remaining 50%
- TP3 (Point A): Oldest trendline touch point - optional extended target
- No 100% certainty price reaches all levels; react to the market
- Move stop-loss to breakeven or to the last higher low/lower high as price progresses
- Can re-enter on retests of broken levels (e.g., price breaks Point B, retests it, enter again)
- Sniper entry: Enter on doji candlestick formation at the retest zone

MULTI-TF RULES:
- Strategy works on every timeframe (15m, 1H, 4H, Daily)
- Author personally trades on 1H or 4H timeframes
- Daily TF used for cleaner chart examples, but not the trading TF
- Higher TF = trades take longer (daily trades take months)
