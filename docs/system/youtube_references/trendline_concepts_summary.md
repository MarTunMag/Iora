## Key Consensus Points (Across All 6 Videos)

1. **Trendlines are context, not entry signals.** Draw them to understand structure and identify where liquidity/orders accumulate, but don't trade blindly from them.

2. **Never enter on the break alone.** All videos emphasize waiting for retest + confirmation. False breakouts are the #1 trap.

3. **Supply/Demand zones provide the actual entry.** The intersection of a broken trendline with an S/D zone is the highest-probability setup across all videos.

4. **Real market structure (HH/HL, LH/LL) overrides trendlines.** A trendline can break while the trend remains intact. Always check the structural high/low.

5. **Confirmation is mandatory.** Rejection candles (pin bars, engulfing, doji, long wicks) at the retest zone confirm the entry. A reaction alone is not enough.

6. **Multi-timeframe alignment increases probability.** Use HTF for direction and key levels, LTF for refined entries. Most suggest 1-2 TF steps down.

7. **Minimum 2:1 risk-reward.** Stop loss beyond the zone; target the next structural level. Zone refinement (V23) can dramatically improve R:R.

8. **Trendlines as inducement.** V20 and V22 specifically frame trendlines as areas that attract retail orders (both retest sellers and breakout buyers), creating liquidity for institutional moves.
