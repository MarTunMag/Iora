TRENDLINE DRAWING RULES:
- Connect swing highs (downtrend) or swing lows (uptrend) - minimum 2 touches required
- More touches = stronger trend line, but more touches don't guarantee it holds next time
- Draw trend line where you get the most touches (through wicks, bodies, or both)
- Treat trend lines as zones, not exact lines - price won't respect them perfectly every time
- Don't force trend lines - if no logical trend line exists, leave it out
- In downtrend: trend line above price connecting swing highs (dynamic resistance)
- In uptrend: trend line below price connecting swing lows (dynamic support)

TRENDLINE BREAK RULES:
- Trend line breaks can signal trend reversals, but NOT guaranteed
- A break alone is NOT enough - need extra confirmation
- Look for momentum loss BEFORE the break (double top/bottom, failure to make new high/low)
- Double rejection pattern before breakout = extra confidence
- False breakouts are common - wait for confirmation to avoid them
- Liquidity sweeps often happen at the breakout level (designed to grab stops then reverse)
- Breakout + double top/bottom + liquidity sweep = strong reversal setup

ZONE-TO-TRENDLINE INTERACTION:
- High Confluence zones: where trend line intersects with support/resistance level
- Look left on chart for previous S/R levels that align with the trend line
- Supply zones above trend line / demand zones below trend line = strongest setups
- Liquidity sweeps at trend line = stops triggered, then reversal (trapping breakout traders)
- Order blocks that become breaker blocks at trendline breakout areas
- Breaker block rejection + trendline break = high probability entry

PIVOT IDENTIFICATION RULES:
- Swing high: highest point before a pullback starts
- Swing low: lowest point before the next run begins
- Uptrend: higher highs and higher lows
- Downtrend: lower highs and lower lows
- Market structure (HH/HL or LH/LL) determines true trend, not the trend line itself
- Double top: market fails to make new high = momentum loss
- Double bottom: market fails to make new low = buyers defending

ENTRY/EXIT RULES AROUND TRENDLINE BREAKS:
- Trend Line Bounce Strategy (trend-following):
  - Wait for price to pull back to trend line
  - Wait for price action confirmation (bullish engulfing, bearish pin bar, rejection candles)
  - Draw short-term trend line on the pullback; wait for break of that short-term TL
  - Enter at close of confirmation candle
  - Stop loss: beyond the confirmation candle wick
  - Target: next key S/R level, minimum 2:1 R:R
- Trend Line Breakout Strategy (reversal):
  - Look for momentum loss first (failure to make new high/low, double top/bottom)
  - Wait for trend line break
  - Wait for retest of broken trend line
  - Confirmation: rejection at retest, engulfing candle, liquidity sweep
  - Enter at close of confirmation candle
  - Stop loss: above/below rejection candle wick
  - Target: next S/R level, 2:1 R:R minimum

MULTI-TF RULES:
- Top-down analysis is crucial - always align with higher TF trend
- 5m/15m trading TF -> 1H higher TF
- 1H trading TF -> Daily higher TF
- 4H trading TF -> Weekly higher TF
- Daily trading TF -> Monthly higher TF
- Use HTF to spot overall trend and key levels, LTF to refine entries
- Confirm trendline breaks on both trading TF and higher TF
- HTF order blocks, breaker blocks, and liquidity sweeps add confluence to LTF setups
- Example: 5m trendline break + 1H trendline break + 1H breaker block rejection = high probability
